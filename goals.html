Future goals

Where I'm headed

Electrical and Computer Engineering, then a career where math and fast hardware meet: low-latency trading and hardware development.

The plan

The next ten years

Plans change, but these are the directions I'm working toward.

  1. Now · Grade 12

    Finish strong at Mackenzie

    Keep my marks high, finish the occupancy counter for the Fitness Club, and compete at CPU Nationals in February 2027.

  2. University

    Electrical & Computer Engineering

    Electrical Engineering is my first choice. I want a program heavy in math and hands-on design: circuits, embedded systems, signals and computer architecture. I'm applying to both Electrical Engineering and Computer Engineering.

  3. Career

    Fast systems, real hardware

    I'm drawn to high-frequency trading and low-latency quantitative work, where math, hardware and software are tuned to shave off microseconds. Hardware development more broadly interests me too: designing the circuits and systems that make that speed possible.

  4. In ten years

    A balanced life

    My activities have taught me I need balance. I see myself as a mature professional who still plays guitar and lifts, and who is starting to build a family, bringing the same discipline and responsibility home.

University

Why Electrical & Computer Engineering

Math that does something

Math is my favourite subject, and I worked through calculus in my enrichment program. Engineering is where it turns into something you can hold.

I already build

An ESP32 and mmWave occupancy counter, 3D-printed FTC robot parts and Arduino projects have shown me how much I enjoy making hardware work.

Clear feedback

A circuit works or it doesn't. That's the same honesty I like about powerlifting and contest math.
