My story

My name is Zachary, and I am a Grade 12 student at William Lyon Mackenzie C.I. I love math, athletics, and art. Of these, my biggest strength is math. I like taking a complicated problem and finding the steps required to make it solvable.

I may sometimes complain that my schedule is busy. Many of my peers ask why I don't drop some of the additional things that I do. Over the past few years in high school, I've realized that I actually thrive under a busy schedule. By constantly challenging myself in so many ways, I find that every day has a new purpose, and I would not like to live any other way.

My parents have always lived by the idea that you should be capable in as many ways as possible. This is why I believe it is important to challenge yourself in so many different ways. On top of my activities, I have been making sure that I am a capable cook, shopper, budgeter, and more.

Where I come from

Both of my parents were born in Bulgaria, and I grew up speaking both English and Bulgarian. I've been part of the Golden Thrace Bulgarian Folk Ensemble for 12 years, since it was founded. Today I help lead 20 young dancers and coach newcomers, and we've performed for audiences of up to 800 people in Toronto and Montreal. We even met Toronto's mayor through an event!

How I think

The thread through everything I do is that I like problems with clear feedback. A proof is right or it isn't. A circuit works or it doesn't. The bar goes up or it stays on the floor. That is exactly what draws me to mathematics and electronics, and it's why I want to study Electrical Engineering.
