My story

Hi, I'm Zach. I'm a Grade 12 student at William Lyon Mackenzie Collegiate Institute. Math is my favourite subject, and I love athletics and the arts just as much.

People sometimes ask why I do so many different things. The honest answer is that I want to keep challenging myself in as many areas as I can. Whether I'm working through a contest problem, wiring up a microcontroller, lifting, playing on a team, or performing dance and music, I do best when something is hard and I have to work through it.

Where I come from

My family's roots are in Bulgaria, and I grew up speaking both English and Bulgarian. Every Friday and Sunday I'm at folk dance practice, learning dances that have been passed down for generations. Performing them on stage connects me to my culture and has made me far more confident in front of an audience.

How I think

The thread through everything I do is that I like problems with clear feedback. A proof is right or it isn't. A circuit works or it doesn't. The bar goes up or it stays on the floor. That honesty is what draws me to mathematics and electronics, and it's why I want to study Electrical Engineering.

What every activity taught me

Discipline

Humility

On the rugby pitch and the robotics team, nothing works unless everyone does their part.

Drive

I pick activities that scare me a little, because that is where I grow fastest.

Expression

Music and dance let me say things that equations and circuits can't.

Resilience

Rugby taught me to take a hit and keep moving forward. That carries into everything else.
