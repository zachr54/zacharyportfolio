My story

Hi, I'm Zach. I'm a Grade 12 student at William Lyon Mackenzie Collegiate Institute. Math is my favourite subject, and I love athletics and the arts just as much.

People sometimes ask why I do so many different things. The honest answer is that I want to keep challenging myself in as many areas as I can. In a typical week I might be tutoring a classmate in calculus, running a shift at the pool, training for powerlifting nationals, practising guitar, and wiring up an ESP32. I do best when something is hard and I have to work through it.

Where I come from

My family's roots are in Bulgaria, and I grew up speaking both English and Bulgarian. I've been part of the Golden Thrace Bulgarian Folk Ensemble for 12 years, since it was founded. Today I lead 20 young dancers and coach newcomers, and we've performed for audiences of up to 800 people in Toronto and Montreal. We even met Toronto's mayor.

How I think

The thread through everything I do is that I like problems with clear feedback. A proof is right or it isn't. A circuit works or it doesn't. The bar goes up or it stays on the floor. That honesty is what draws me to mathematics and electronics, and it's why I want to study Electrical Engineering.

What every activity taught me

Discipline

Powerlifting and guitar both reward the person who shows up on the days they don't feel like it.

Responsibility

Running a pool shift with 10 staff and up to 225 swimmers teaches you to stay calm and own every decision.

Teamwork

As rugby vice captain and on a 12-person robotics team, nothing works unless everyone does their part.

Service

Tutoring, coaching younger dancers and growing the Fitness Club: I like helping other people get better.

Expression

Music and dance let me say things that equations and circuits can't.

Resilience

Rugby taught me to take a hit and keep moving forward. That carries into everything else.
