My story

Hi, I'm Zach. I'm a Grade 12 student who loves academics, especially Mathematics and Computer Science, and who also loves athletics and the arts. People sometimes ask why I do so many different things. The honest answer is that I want to keep challenging myself in as many areas as I can.

Whether I'm working through a contest problem, writing Java, lifting weights, playing on a team, or performing dance and music, I do best when something is hard and I have to work through it. Those experiences have taught me discipline, humility, teamwork, drive and expression, and together they shape how I approach the rest of my life.

Where I come from

My family's roots are in Bulgaria, and that heritage is a big part of who I am. Every Friday and Sunday I'm at folk dance practice, learning the same dances that have been passed down for generations. Performing them on stage connects me to my culture and has made me far more confident in front of an audience.

How I think

The common thread through everything I do is that I like problems with clear feedback. A proof is right or it isn't. A program compiles or it doesn't. The bar goes up or it stays on the floor. That honesty is what draws me to mathematics and computing, and it's why I want to study them at university.
